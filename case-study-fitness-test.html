Guidance by Design

How I Made a 30-Minute Fitness Test Feel Effortless

Results

  • Delivered a clear, structured flow that guided users confidently through all stages of the 30-minute test.
  • Improved comprehension and engagement, reducing drop-off during longer exercise phases.
  • Designed an intuitive results screen that helped users understand their fitness level and how it influences their personalized activity recommendations.
  • The feature became a key motivator for users to start tailored training plans within the app, linking measurable progress with personalized guidance.

Situation

Client: Germany’s largest health insurance

As part of a health coaching platform, a Fitness Test feature was developed to help users assess their fitness level based on exercises and health data such as heart rate. The test consisted of 15 exercises covering endurance, strength, and flexibility, and required periodic heart rate measurements.

It was one of the most popular features mentioned in a user satisfaction survey, but its complexity — combining instructions, physical activity, and data input — made smooth guidance through the process crucial.

Task

Position: Lead UX Designer

Focus: User Flow Design, Prototyping, User Testing

Design a seamless and motivating user experience that guides users through a 30-minute fitness assessment, including exercise instructions, progress tracking, and heart rate input, while maintaining engagement and clarity throughout the process.

Actions

  • Joined the project after the health concept and requirements were defined to design the end-to-end user flow for the test experience.
  • Conducted user research and interviews to understand expectations, potential confusion points, and motivational factors during longer physical tasks.
  • Designed wireframes and interactive prototypes to test how users navigate between exercises, progress prompts, and heart rate measurements.
  • Focused on clarity of instructions and timing, ensuring the interface provided step-by-step guidance without overwhelming users.
  • Created visual and verbal cues that encouraged users to continue through the test, even during rest phases or repeated measurements.
  • Conducted user testing sessions to validate usability, pacing, and comprehension of exercise explanations and results presentation.

Key learnings

  • Long and complex interactions benefit from step-by-step guidance and motivational feedback to maintain engagement.
  • Users value clarity and reassurance during physically demanding tasks, making tone and pacing critical to UX success.
